# Privacy Notice

LabBook is the department's system for booking shared lab equipment.

## What we collect

- Your **name** and **email address**.
- Your **booking history**: what you booked, when, and its status, including late cancellations and no-shows.
- Records of equipment check-out and check-in, including condition notes and missing accessories.

## Why

To run fair bookings: preventing double-booking, applying quotas and no-show penalties, contacting you about your bookings, and reporting on how equipment is used.

## Who can see it

- **Students** see only their own booking details. Other people's bookings appear as "Booked".
- **Teachers, lab technicians and admins** see names on bookings so they can approve, hand over and follow up on equipment.

## How long we keep it

Bookings, check records and no-show records older than **2 years** are anonymised: your name is removed and only the statistics remain. In-app notifications are deleted after 90 days.

## Your data and requests

To get a copy of your data or to have your account deleted, contact the department's LabBook admin.

*An admin should edit this notice to add the contact details.*
